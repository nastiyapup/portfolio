02 About

Creating stories through motion

I am Anastasiia Pupyrina, a Warsaw-based motion designer and cinematographer currently studying Multimedia and Graphic Design at the Polish-Japanese Academy of Information Technology (PJATK).

My practice spans cinematic video, 3D animation, motion design, game prototyping, digital illustration, and AI-powered content creation. As a FLEX Program alumna and a content creator with nearly 1 million TikTok likes, I explore cultural differences, language learning, and design, while sharing my personal reflections on emotions and human relationships.

I thrive in collaborative, international environments. My cross-cultural projects range from producing a promotional broadcast for a US high school to designing an immersive spatial experience for the DodekaOTTO exhibition in St. Pölten, Austria, during my Erasmus exchange.
